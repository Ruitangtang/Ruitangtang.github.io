## Summary

This study derives 92 glacier surface-speed fields from Sentinel-1 observations between 2014 and 2019. It characterizes regional patterns and seasonal and interannual variations in glacier motion across the Kenai Peninsula.

The work provides a consistent regional velocity record and establishes the observational basis for subsequent analysis of glacier speed–runoff relationships.
