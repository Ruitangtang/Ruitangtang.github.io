## Summary

This study quantifies glacier area and mass change across more than 4,000 km² of glacierized terrain on the Kenai Peninsula between 1986 and 2016. Multi-temporal satellite imagery is used to map glacier outlines, while digital elevation models support estimates of surface-elevation, volume, and mass change.

The results establish a regional baseline for understanding multi-decadal glacier retreat and mass loss in south-central Alaska.
