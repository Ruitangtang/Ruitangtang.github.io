## Summary

This community inter-comparison evaluates glacier elevation-change estimates derived from **ASTER** optical stereo imagery and **TanDEM-X** radar data. Twelve research groups submitted 97 elevation-change datasets for five test sites using different processing strategies.

The study identifies scene selection, digital-elevation-model processing, and co-registration as major sources of variation and highlights the value of open workflows, ensemble estimates, and consistent uncertainty assessment.
