## Summary

This study combines airborne imaging spectroscopy with Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery to map glacier-derived turbidity in Kachemak Bay, Alaska. High-resolution spectral information is used to develop and evaluate a scalable method for tracking sediment-plume extent and seasonal variability.

The work improves understanding of how glacier runoff influences coastal water conditions in a data-sparse region.
