## Class Adviser

**Three years · Approximately 60 students**

Alongside my mathematics teaching, I served as a class adviser for three years, supporting the academic development, organization, and well-being of approximately 60 students.

My responsibilities included:

- Monitoring academic progress, attendance, and student development.
- Providing guidance on study planning, educational goals, and personal development.
- Coordinating communication among students, parents, teachers, and school administrators.
- Supporting classroom organization, student well-being, and conflict resolution.
- Organizing class meetings and contributing to school activities.

## Teaching Approach

My teaching emphasizes conceptual understanding, structured reasoning, and independent problem solving. I aim to create a supportive learning environment in which students can build confidence, ask questions, and develop effective learning strategies.

This experience strengthened my ability to communicate technical ideas clearly, mentor individuals with different needs, lead large groups, and coordinate effectively with multiple stakeholders.
