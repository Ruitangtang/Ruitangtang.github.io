I am a **Researcher** in glacier modeling, data assimilation, and remote sensing. 


## Research Interests

- **Glacier Evolution Modeling** — OGGM, PyGEM, SERMeQ, ice dynamics, climatic mass balance, runoff, frontal ablation
- **Data Assimilation** — Bayesian calibration (ensemble-based), joint parameter estimation, uncertainty quantification
- **Remote Sensing** — Satellite observations of glacier change (area change, surface elevation change, surface ice velocity)
- **Open Science** — Reproducible research, open-source software, Zenodo archiving, open datasets
