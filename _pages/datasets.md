I develop and publish open research datasets supporting glacier observation, model calibration, uncertainty analysis, and future projections. The datasets below are organized by research program.

## Kenai Peninsula, Alaska

### Glacier Mass and Area Changes, 1986–2016

A regional dataset documenting glacier outlines, area change, surface-elevation change, volume change, and mass change across the Kenai Peninsula.

- **Region:** Kenai Peninsula, Alaska
- **Period:** 1986–2016
- **Data sources:** Optical satellite imagery, ASTER stereo imagery, digital elevation models, and satellite altimetry
- **Repository:** PANGAEA
- **[Access the dataset](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.965738)**
- **[Related publication](https://doi.org/10.1017/jog.2020.32)**
- **[Related project](/projects/kenai-glaciers/)**

### Glacier Velocity Maps, 2014–2019

Regional glacier surface-velocity maps derived from Sentinel-1 synthetic aperture radar observations using offset-tracking methods.

- **Region:** Kenai Peninsula, Alaska
- **Period:** 2014–2019
- **Variables:** Glacier surface velocity and associated spatial and temporal information
- **Repository:** PANGAEA
- **[Access the dataset](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.971786)**
- **[Related publication](https://doi.org/10.1029/2022JF006599)**
- **[Related project](/projects/kenai-glaciers/)**

### Glacier Surface Speed and Runoff, 2014–2019

A regional dataset supporting analysis of seasonal and interannual relationships between glacier surface speed and modeled runoff.

- **Region:** Kenai Peninsula, Alaska
- **Period:** 2014–2019
- **Variables:** Glacier surface speed, runoff, and supporting glacier-scale information
- **Repository:** Mendeley Data
- **[Access the dataset](https://doi.org/10.17632/g3s7m78zk9.1)**
- **[Related publication](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025GL115248)**
- **[Related project](/projects/kenai-glaciers/)**

## Svalbard

### Marine-Terminating Glacier Calibration and Projection Dataset

A model-derived dataset containing calibration, evaluation, parameter, and projection outputs from the coupled **OGGM–PyGEM–SERMeQ** framework.

- **Region:** Svalbard
- **Coverage:** 71 marine-terminating glaciers
- **Simulation period:** Historical simulations（2000-2020） and projections through 2100
- **Variables:** Model parameters, calibration outputs, evaluation results, and glacier projections
- **Repository:** NIRD RDA
- **[Access the dataset](https://doi.org/10.11582/2026.7whvas95)**
- **[Related preprint](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-1081)**
- **[Software archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18761729)**
- **[Related project](/projects/frontalablation-modeling/)**
