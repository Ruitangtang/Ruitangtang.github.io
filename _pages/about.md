I am a **cryosphere scientist and researcher at the University of Oslo**, specializing in glacier modeling, data assimilation, and satellite remote sensing. I develop observation-driven modeling systems that integrate process-based models, Earth-observation data, and Bayesian inference to quantify glacier change and reduce uncertainty in projections of glacier evolution.

I hold a PhD from the **University of Chinese Academy of Sciences**. My doctoral training included 18 months as a visiting PhD researcher at the **University of Alaska Fairbanks** and one year at the **University of Oslo**, where I have worked as a researcher since 2022.

My research spans glacier dynamics, climatic mass balance, frontal ablation, and glacier–climate interactions. I use optical satellite imagery, synthetic aperture radar, and satellite altimetry to measure changes in glacier area, surface elevation, mass, and ice velocity.

My work on the **Kenai Peninsula, Alaska**, is a sustained Earth-observation research program examining multi-decadal changes in glacier area, mass, and surface velocity, together with the relationships among glacier motion, atmospheric forcing, and meltwater runoff. This work has resulted in **three peer-reviewed publications and three published regional datasets**.

I also led the design and development of a coupled modeling and data-assimilation framework for marine-terminating glaciers that integrates **OGGM**, **PyGEM**, and **SERMeQ**. The framework combines climatic mass balance, glacier evolution, ice dynamics, and frontal ablation with joint Bayesian calibration to assimilate multiple observational constraints, estimate uncertain parameters, and quantify projection uncertainty. It has been applied to **71 marine-terminating glaciers in Svalbard** for historical simulations and projections through 2100.

Across these projects, I design reproducible computational workflows for multi-source geospatial data integration, model calibration, uncertainty analysis, and regional-to-global simulation. My broader goal is to develop rigorous and scalable methods that improve glacier projections and support climate science, sea-level assessment, freshwater-resource evaluation, and environmental monitoring.

## Research Interests

- **Glacier Modeling and Projection** — Glacier evolution, ice dynamics, climatic mass balance, runoff, and regional-to-global simulation.
