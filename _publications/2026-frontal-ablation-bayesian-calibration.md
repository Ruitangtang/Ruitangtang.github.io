## Summary

This study presents a coupled and computationally efficient framework that integrates **SERMeQ** with **OGGM** and **PyGEM** to simulate climatic mass balance, glacier evolution, ice dynamics, and frontal ablation. Joint Bayesian calibration combines multiple observational constraints to estimate uncertain parameters and propagate uncertainty into glacier projections.

The framework is demonstrated for 71 marine-terminating glaciers in Svalbard using historical simulations and projections through 2100.
