## Summary

This study examines relationships between glacier surface speed and modeled runoff for 77 glaciers on the Kenai Peninsula. It evaluates how seasonal speed patterns vary among glaciers and how year-to-year runoff variability influences glacier motion.

The analysis links satellite-derived velocity observations with hydrological modeling to investigate glacier–runoff interactions at regional scale.
