## Summary

This data article documents multi-year glaciological and meteorological observations from debris-covered Kennicott Glacier in Alaska. The dataset includes point mass-balance measurements, meteorological records, debris thickness and temperature, ice-cliff change, and observations of proglacial and supraglacial lakes.

The openly published record supports glacier-process studies and the calibration and evaluation of glacier models.
