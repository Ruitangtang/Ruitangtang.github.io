I am a **cryosphere researcher** specializing in glacier modeling, data assimilation, and satellite remote sensing. I combine physical models, Earth-observation data, and statistical inference to understand glacier change and improve projections of glacier evolution in a warming climate.

I hold a PhD from the **University of Chinese Academy of Sciences** and have international research experience at the **University of Alaska Fairbanks** and the **University of Oslo**.

My research spans glacier dynamics, mass balance, frontal ablation, and glacier–climate interactions. I use optical, radar, and satellite-altimetry observations to quantify changes in glacier area, surface elevation, mass, and ice velocity.

I led the development of a coupled modeling workflow for marine-terminating glaciers that integrates **OGGM**, **PyGEM**, and **SERMeQ**. The workflow connects climatic mass balance, glacier evolution, ice dynamics, and frontal ablation, supporting regional-to-global simulations and the integration of multiple observational datasets.

I also develop data-assimilation and model-calibration methods to combine in situ measurements and remote-sensing observations, estimate uncertain parameters, and quantify projection uncertainty.

## Research Interests

- **Glacier Modeling** — Glacier evolution, ice dynamics, climatic mass balance, runoff, and regional-to-global projections.
- **Frontal Ablation** — Calving, marine-terminating glacier dynamics, and frontal ice loss in large-scale models.
- **Data Assimilation** — Bayesian calibration, parameter estimation, model–observation integration, and uncertainty quantification.
- **Satellite Remote Sensing** — Glacier area, elevation change, mass change, and ice velocity derived from optical, radar, and altimetry observations.
- **Glacier–Climate Interactions** — Atmospheric forcing, surface mass balance, meltwater runoff, and glacier response.
- **Open Science** — Reproducible workflows, open-source software, reusable datasets, and long-term research archiving.
